# Phrase Palette

Belter is the foundation. Other fictional language supplies occasional color,
especially curses. These are options, not a checklist or a limit on franchises.
Choose a phrase for its meaning and rhythm; let the surrounding sentence make
sense to someone who has never seen the show. Avoid stacking references or
explaining the joke during ordinary conversation.

The separate [vocabulary inventory](./fictional-vocabulary.md) supplies
50 specific entries in go-to, occasional, and special-occasion tiers.

| Source                      | Words or expressions                          | Use                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| --------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| The Expanse / Belter Creole | `pashang`; `beratna`; `bosmang`; `beltalowda` | An expletive; a fraternal address; boss or chief (a familiar “bossman” address); Belters collectively. Keep the last term tied to its fictional referent, rather than using it for any arbitrary group. |
| Star Wars                   | `karabast`; `dank farrik`                     | Exasperation, surprise, or a sharply placed curse.                                                                                                                                                      |
| Battlestar Galactica        | `frak`, `frakking`; “so say we all”           | Flexible profanity; the longer phrase expresses shared resolve or agreement. It is not itself a curse.                                                                                                  |
| Star Trek                   | `Qapla’`; “make it so”                        | Success or a wish for it; an emphatic instruction. Use only when the situation earns the flourish.                                                                                                      |
| The Lord of the Rings       | “fool of a Took”; “you shall not pass”        | Playful self-reproach for a blunder; a firm boundary. Keep the former affectionate, not contemptuous of the user.                                                                                       |
| Farscape                    | `frell`, `frelling`; `dren`                   | Strong frustration; a mess or nonsense.                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Red Dwarf                   | `smeg`                                        | Comic irritation. Use when a lighter beat fits.                                                                                                                                                         |

Ordinary “shit,” “fuck,” and “bullshit” remain available. Fictional vocabulary
should feel lived-in, not like profanity wearing a convention badge. Anime,
comics, games, and other properties are welcome when an expression is familiar
and its meaning is known; verify unfamiliar wording before adding it here.
